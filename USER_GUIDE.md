# Preschool Software — Simple User Guide

This guide is written for school administrators and staff who do not work with software every day.

## Start here

After signing in, open **Start here** in the left menu. It is a launchpad for the main tasks. You can search using everyday words such as *child*, *money*, *attendance*, or *report card*. You can also use **Find a page or task** in the top bar, or press **Ctrl+K** on a Windows computer.

## Take attendance

1. Open **Take attendance**.
2. Select the correct date and class.
3. Mark each child present or absent. Add a reason for an absence when asked.
4. If attendance has already been saved for that date, the saved statuses load automatically. Correct what needs changing and save again; existing entries are updated instead of duplicated.
4. Check the class and date once more.
5. Save and wait for the confirmation message. If you change the class or date after making unsaved changes, the screen asks before discarding them.

## Register a child

1. Open **Students** and choose the add/register option.
2. Enter the child's name, class, date of birth and required family details.
3. Check phone numbers and spellings. Add photos when available and appropriate.
4. Save the record.
5. Return to the student list and check that the new record appears.

## Record a fee payment

1. Open **Fees & payments**.
2. Find the correct child's record. Verify the child's name and class before changing any amount.
3. Enter the amount received and check the date and discount, if applicable.
4. Review the amount due after payment.
5. Save once and wait for confirmation. Avoid repeatedly pressing Save if the page is still processing.

## Print a fee payment receipt

1. Open **Fees & payments** and find the correct child.
2. Check the child’s name, GR number and class before opening the receipt.
3. Choose **Receipt** on that fee record.
4. If the child has multiple saved payments, choose the payment you need.
5. Review the payment date, amount and student details, then choose **Print receipt**.
6. In the print dialog, select your printer or choose “Save as PDF”.

Receipts are generated only from saved payment entries. The displayed balance is the current balance, so it may include payments recorded after an older payment. Verify the details before handing a printed copy to a parent or guardian.

## Prepare a report card

1. Open **Report cards** and select or create the relevant class report.
2. Choose the student/report whose marks you want to update.
3. Enter marks carefully against the correct subjects and maximum marks.
4. Save and review totals and student details.
5. Preview the print layout before printing several copies.

## Add a school event

1. Open **School calendar**.
2. Select the event date and add a clear event title.
3. Review the date and time and save the event.
4. Return to the calendar to confirm it is displayed on the expected date.

## Add classroom material or photos

Use **PDF resources** for PDF/worksheet material, **Video resources** for videos, and **Photo gallery** for school activity photos. Add a useful title and the class/subject information available on the form. After saving, check that the new item appears in the list.

## Share or read school notices

**Administrators**
1. Open **School notices** from the menu or use the task finder.
2. Choose **Write a notice** and enter a clear title and simple message.
3. Choose the audience (all staff or teachers), importance and start date. You can optionally choose when the notice should stop appearing.
4. Preview the details, then choose **Publish notice**.
5. Use **Edit** to correct a message or **Archive** to hide an old notice without permanently deleting its history.

**Teachers**
1. Open **School notices** in the teacher workspace.
2. Read the latest active notices and check any dates or instructions.
3. Contact the administrator if a notice seems incorrect or unclear.

The notice board is internal to this software. It does not send WhatsApp, SMS or email messages.

## If something goes wrong

- **Can't sign in:** check spelling and Caps Lock. Ask the administrator to verify your account if it persists.
- **Page loading or error:** confirm the internet connection, note the page and exact error, and contact the software administrator.
- **Can't find a record:** clear list filters and check the class/date. Verify whether the save succeeded before creating another record.
- **Unsure before editing or deleting:** cancel and ask the administrator. Never share passwords.

## For organization-workspace users: export a report

1. Open **Reports & exports** from the organization workspace.
2. Select the branch in the top selector before exporting. The report is always limited to that branch.
3. Choose the **From** and **To** dates. The range can be up to 366 days.
4. Choose **Download attendance CSV** for attendance date, class, admission number, child name, status and absence reason.
5. Choose **Download fee CSV** for saved payments and refunds, receipt/invoice references, amount, payment method and refund reason. Only users with fee-report permission see this option.
6. Open the downloaded CSV in Excel or Google Sheets and verify the branch and dates before sharing it.

Report exports are recorded in the audit history. Teachers can export only attendance for their assigned classes. These reports are separate from the legacy single-school report pages; public organization registration remains disabled while other legacy modules are being migrated.

## Safe daily habits

- Confirm the child's name and class before changing attendance or fees.
- Check dates and amounts before saving.
- Wait for the on-screen confirmation before moving to another task.
- Follow the school's privacy policy for family contact details and photos.
- Ask an administrator before making uncertain corrections to financial or student records.

The guide explains common workflows, but the school's approved fee policy and record-retention procedures always take precedence.
